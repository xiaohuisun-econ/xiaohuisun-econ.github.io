Xiaohui Sun

Pronounced “shyow-hway”

Ph.D. Candidate in Economics
Rutgers University

  • Irvine, CA
  • xiaohui.sun@rutgers.edu

My research examines how economic policies and risks shape firm and household decisions, with a focus on firm dynamics, precautionary saving, and retirement. I combine large-scale microdata, econometric methods, and quantitative structural models to study these questions.

Before my Ph.D., I worked at the International Monetary Fund for three years.

Research interests

  • Macroeconomics
  • Firm Dynamics
  • Labor Economics
